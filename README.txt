Deployment
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Upload the contents of the vanta-aec-website folder into public_html on Hostinger.
3. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
4. Upload the eight original PDF files into assets/pdfs/ using the exact filenames:
   MachenicalShop.pdf, SauceHouse.pdf, ParisBaquette.pdf, DanielTeel.pdf,
   PinnacleMontessoriSchool.pdf, WatermillExpressCity.pdf, FreshmanHill.pdf,
   FiveStarCarWash.pdf.
5. Upload your favicon as favicon.png in the website root, or change the icon path.
6. Test the inquiry form. FormSubmit may require a one-time activation email to info@vanta-aec.com.

Important content checks before going live
- The old website's statistics (1,534+, 98%, 15+, 24/7) are retained as supplied claims. Confirm they are accurate and approved.
- The old website named Michael Wilson as CEO; confirm this name and title before publishing.
- Project gallery images are illustrative stock photography, not verified VANTA AEC project photos. Replace them with real project photos or label them as illustrative.
- Phone number and email were retained from the supplied website. Verify both.
- The old source included no actual PDF files, only links. Add the original PDFs to assets/pdfs/.
- Images currently load from images.unsplash.com. For best speed and reliability, download optimized images and serve them locally when ready.

SEO
- Page-specific titles and descriptions are included.
- Canonical URLs point to vanta-aec.com.
- Organization structured data is included with the contact details supplied in the source.
